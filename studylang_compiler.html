Grammar Guide

✏️ How to write instructions

You can write naturally! Filler words like i want to, today, gonna etc. are silently ignored. The compiler picks out the meaningful words.

Minimum pattern: ACTION + SUBJECT

Full pattern:

[MODIFIER] ACTION SUBJECT [for N UNIT] [PREP EVENT/TIME] [on DAY] [PRIORITY]
